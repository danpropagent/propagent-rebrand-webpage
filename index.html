Start with the document you need to understand.

RFP quality

Inside the report

Twelve dimensions, plus the risks between them.

The RFP-quality report evaluates the following dimensions and adds ambiguity, killer-clause, improvement-priority, and bid/no-bid context.

  1. Scope and deliverables precision
  2. Evaluation criteria transparency
  3. Technical requirements and drawings
  4. Fair competition and true discriminators
  5. Purpose, outcomes, and project context
  6. Schedule realism and milestones
  7. Internal consistency
  8. Commercial terms and risk allocation
  9. Innovation flexibility and trade-offs
  10. Compliance requirements
  11. Submission instructions and format
  12. Communication and Q&A process
Human decision

A sharper read, not an automatic verdict.

The grader organizes evidence, gaps, and pursuit risk so a proposal leader, capture team, procurement team, or executive can decide what to fix, clarify, pursue, or decline.

FAQ

How the RFP Grader works.

What is the Propagent RFP Grader?

It is a free tool for assessing an RFP's quality or comparing a draft response against the source RFP. It emails a structured report for human review.

What can I grade?

Upload an RFP for a quality and clarity assessment, or upload an RFP with a draft response for a compliance and fit comparison.

What does the RFP report cover?

The report covers 12 dimensions: scope precision, evaluation transparency, technical requirements, fair competition, project context, schedule realism, internal consistency, commercial risk, innovation flexibility, compliance requirements, submission instructions, and the communication process.

Does it make the final bid or no-bid decision?

No. It provides structured decision support. Your proposal, capture, procurement, or leadership team makes the final decision.

Which files can I upload?

PDF, DOCX, and TXT files are supported, with a 20 MB combined limit per submission.

How are submitted files used?

Your email address and uploaded files are submitted to Propagent to generate and deliver the requested report. See the security and procurement guide.
